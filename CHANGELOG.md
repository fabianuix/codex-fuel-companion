# Changelog

## 1.05 · September 8, 2026

More ways to keep Codex usage close, with a cleaner look.

### Added

- Menu-bar choices: remaining percentage, time until reset, or icon only.
- Live reset countdowns alongside the existing date-and-time option.
- A pinned panel you can drag anywhere, with a remembered position.
- Presentation mode to hide balances and silence usage alerts while sharing your screen.
- Allowance alerts at 25%, 10%, both, or a custom percentage.
- An optional reasoning badge for your most recently used Codex task, with matching colors.

### Refined

- Cleaner plan badges, icons, tooltips, and Settings.
- Matching blur and dimming behind tooltips and app dialogs.
- The panel starts unpinned on each launch.
- “Return panel to menu bar” now lives in the menu-bar icon’s right-click menu.
- Updated screenshots and a clearer guide to the app’s features.

## 1.04

- Refreshed app artwork and gauge icon.
- Improved keyboard-shortcut recording and focus handling.
- A compact update badge and centered update dialogs.
